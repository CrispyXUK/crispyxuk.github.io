Bridget Walker absconded from Sweyne Court and was found on Rayleigh High Street barefoot. CQC and the safeguarding lead were both notified of the incident.

A 24-hour 1:1 care arrangement was subsequently imposed. The family was billed £4,460.65 for this provision.
