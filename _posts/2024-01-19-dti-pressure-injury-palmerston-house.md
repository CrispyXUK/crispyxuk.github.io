A Deep Tissue Injury (DTI) to the left buttock was identified. A DATIX incident report (reference **E259548**) was completed by EPUT District Nursing on 18 January 2024. A dynamic mattress and cushion were ordered, repositioning every 2 hours was prescribed, and SEPSIS monitoring initiated. Waterlow score recorded at 17.

The DATIX report was not disclosed in the SAR response. Attorneys were not notified at the time. The incident was identified from EPUT clinical records.

This is a reportable pressure injury triggering a DATIX adverse event report. The failure to notify the attorneys and the non-disclosure of the DATIX in the SAR response are documented in **[Issue 52]({{ '/issues/' | relative_url }}#issue-52)**.
