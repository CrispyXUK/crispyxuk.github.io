## Significance

**1. First documented contact with the Council — 5 years before the formal complaint**

This email establishes that Southend-on-Sea Borough Council was formally notified of Bridget's care needs on 2 April 2020 — more than five years before the formal complaint was submitted. Kira describes wandering behaviour (5 walks per day, knocking on neighbours' doors, unsettled at night), unofficial carers already in place, and explicitly asks whether a care home placement is the next step.

**2. Council response was inadequate — no assessment, no safeguarding referral**

Jessica St John's response is a single paragraph suggesting the GP be contacted and attaching an activity pack. There is no:
- Care Act needs assessment offered or conducted
- Safeguarding referral made
- CHC consideration initiated
- Formal recording of contact with the Access Team
- Any follow-up action promised

This is despite Kira describing a person with advanced dementia, wandering unsupervised five times per day, unable to recognise her own home, and requiring neighbours to be managed. The Council's duty to assess under s.9 Care Act 2014 arose at this point.

**3. Email directed to Kira — Mark copied only**

The Council's response was addressed to Kira Walker. Mark Walker was copied. This is the earliest documented instance of the selective communication pattern that recurs throughout — health and welfare matters directed to Kira, financial matters to Mark, despite both later holding identical joint LPA across both domains. At this point, April 2020, the LPA may not yet have been activated for care purposes, but the pattern is established from the outset.

**4. No record in Council's system**

The Council's own complaint response (March 2026) makes no reference to this April 2020 contact. The response to Mark's complaint in November 2020 (Service Request 6614531) was treated as the starting point of Council involvement. This email predates that by three months and establishes earlier actual knowledge of Bridget's needs.

**5. "Unofficial carers" — family providing unrecognised support**

Kira's email discloses that the family had already arranged unofficial carers during the week. This is a carer's assessment trigger under s.10 Care Act 2014 — the Council had a duty to offer a carer's assessment to Kira. No such assessment was ever offered or conducted.
