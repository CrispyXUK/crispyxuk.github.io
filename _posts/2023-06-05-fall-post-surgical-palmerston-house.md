A further fall was documented by a PCN clinician during a care home visit, 10 days post-discharge from hospital following a right neck of femur fracture (right hip hemiarthroplasty, 15 May 2023). An ambulance attended. Bridget was left mobilising with assistance as her normal baseline.

Attorneys Mark Walker and Kira Walker were not notified. The incident was identified from PCN clinical records, not from any communication by the care home or the Council.

This incident engages **CQC Regulation 16** and the **Duty of Candour (Regulation 20)**. For a resident who lacks capacity, the duty to notify extends to legal representatives — here, both attorneys under the joint LPA. See **[Issue 56]({{ '/issues/' | relative_url }}#issue-56)**.
