## Significance

**1. Confirms the DPA rate structure — and the overcharge arithmetic**

Lynn Hyam's table confirms the Council was paying Palmerston House £750/week and deferring £386.30/week. The family was being billed a total of £877.59/week (contribution £363.70 + deferral £513.89 per the March 2023 financial assessment letter). But the deferred rate given here is £386.30 — not £513.89. These figures do not reconcile. The Council's own internal records show a different deferral rate from the rate quoted to the family. See Issue 19.

**2. Meeting on 16 October 2025 — not previously on record**

This email confirms a meeting took place at the Civic Centre on 16 October 2025, two weeks before the first complaint response. The meeting discussed invoices and the DPA. No record of this meeting has been disclosed in the complaint correspondence. The agenda, attendees, and any agreements reached at this meeting have not been provided. This is a gap in the documentary record.

**3. Invoice date anomaly — "6th April 2023 to 2nd April 2023"**

Lynn Hyam refers to an invoice for "the 4-week period 6th April 2023 to 2nd April 2023." The end date predates the start date — this is either a transcription error in the email or reflects an invoicing error in the original documents. Either way it illustrates the accounting inconsistency that Mark had already identified.

**4. The DPA deferred element varied with care home cost — but was never updated by contract**

The deferred amounts changed each time the care home rate changed (£389.77 at £800/week, £386.30 at £750/week, £415.90 at £800.03/week, £449.27 at £844.83/week). These variations were applied administratively without any formal variation to the DPA. The DPA contract named Westcliff Lodge and set out a specific rate — it was never formally varied to cover Palmerston House or to reflect any change in rates. Charges were being deferred against a charge on 84 Bellhouse Road under a contract that had no legal authority to cover the Palmerston placement. See Issues 23, 24, and 25.

**5. The credit note of 19 June 2023 — the partial double-billing resolution**

Lynn Hyam references a credit note issued 19 June 2023. This is the partial resolution of the double-billing identified when both the family and the Council had paid Westcliff Lodge for overlapping periods. The refund of £9,496.79 was obtained only after the family threatened bailiff action. The credit note is now formally confirmed in Council correspondence. See Issue 28.
