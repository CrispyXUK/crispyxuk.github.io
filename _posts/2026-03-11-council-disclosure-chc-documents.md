## Significance

**1. This is the first time the family ever received any CHC checklist**

All five Council CHC checklists and both ASC assessments were disclosed for the first time on 11 March 2026 — as attachments to the Council's second and final complaint response. The family had not seen any of these documents at any earlier point:

- Not when they were completed (Oct 2022, Feb 2023, Sep 2023, May 2024, Jun 2025)
- Not during the original complaint process
- Not in response to any earlier request

The family had been living with the consequences of negative CHC decisions for over three years before they were shown the documents on which those decisions were based. They could not challenge the incorrect age, the impossible address, or the inadequate domain scores until this disclosure.

**2. The June 2025 checklist PDF was created six days before this disclosure**

The June 2025 CHC checklist (CHC checklist 18 06 25) carries PDF creation metadata of **5 March 2026** — six days before this email. The document was created on 5 March 2026 and first sent to the family on 11 March 2026. A contemporaneous record produced on 18 June 2025 would be expected to carry creation metadata from that date or close to it. The metadata convergence with the disclosure date is consistent with the document having been generated or assembled at the point of disclosure. See Issues 38 and 46.

**3. Every document carries the impossible Palmerston address**

Every checklist and assessment in this bundle records Palmerston House as Bridget's address — including documents dated up to six months before she moved there (11 April 2023). The documents were provided to the family at a point when the Council could not have expected this to go unnoticed. The family spotted the anomaly immediately. See Issues 36 and 37.

**4. The disclosure coincided with the second complaint response**

The second complaint response was also issued on 11 March 2026 (signed Helen Cummings / Michael Barrett). The documents were attached to the same email as the formal complaint response — they were not provided proactively or in advance. The timing means the Council used the same communication to close the complaint and provide the first disclosure of the evidential basis for the CHC decisions. The family had no opportunity to engage with the documents before the complaint was declared exhausted.

**5. The bundle does not include all relevant documents**

The disclosed bundle includes CHC checklists and ICB letters but does not include:
- Any needs assessment conducted at the time of each checklist
- Any care and support plan
- Any financial assessment records
- Any MCA or Best Interests documentation relating to the DPA
- The Individual Placement Contract for Palmerston House
- Any DoLS renewal documentation post-2020
- The July 2024 formal MCA for covert medication

The incompleteness of the disclosure was itself the basis for the formal complaint to the LGSCO. See Issues 31 and 42.
