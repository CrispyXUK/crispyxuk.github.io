A large prolapse was photographed and an intimate image was sent without patient consent or any Mental Capacity Act Best Interests process. The GP issued an urgent instruction for the image to be removed from all records.

Bridget Walker lacks capacity. No Best Interests decision was documented for either the intimate examination or the photography. This constitutes a consent and safeguarding breach under the MCA 2005.

Attorneys were not notified at the time. The incident was identified from clinical records.
