An unwitnessed fall from a chair was captured on CCTV. Bridget sustained right hip and knee bruising and swelling, vomited twice, and was weight-bearing with pain. No head injury or loss of consciousness was recorded.

A DNACPR was completed on the same day as the fall. The fall resulted in a right neck of femur (NOF) fracture requiring hemiarthroplasty. Following hospitalisation and surgery, Bridget was discharged — and sustained a further fall 10 days post-discharge (see [5 June 2023]({{ '/incidents/fall-post-surgical-jun23/' | relative_url }})).

Whether attorneys were notified at the time of the original fall is unknown — the incident was identified from clinical records.
