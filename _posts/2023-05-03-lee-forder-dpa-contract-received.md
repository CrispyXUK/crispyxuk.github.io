## Significance

**1. The DPA contract and CH1 were received by the Council on or around 3 May 2023**

Lee Forder confirms receiving the signed DPA contract and the CH1 form from Kira Walker on approximately 26 April 2023 (she "dropped this off last week"). The DPA was subsequently executed with a stated date of 19 May 2023. The PDF metadata shows the document was scanned on 17 May 2023. This email chain establishes that the physical paperwork was in the Council's possession from approximately 26 April 2023 — three weeks before the stated execution date. See Issues 24 and 39.

**2. Bridget had already moved to Palmerston House when this exchange took place**

At the time of this exchange (3 May 2023), Bridget had been at Palmerston House since 11 April 2023 — 22 days. The DPA contract being processed here named Westcliff Lodge. Lee Forder was processing a contract for a care home placement that had already ended, without raising any query about the incorrect address. The Council's finance officer had actual knowledge of the placement change — the August 2025 Lynn Hyam email confirms the Council was well aware Bridget moved to Palmerston House on 11 April 2023 — yet allowed the defective DPA to proceed. See Issue 23.

**3. "My sister dropped this off last week"**

Kira Walker physically delivered the signed DPA paperwork to the Council. This is relevant to the MCA argument: Kira holds LPA for health and welfare. The DPA is a financial instrument. The fact that Kira delivered the paperwork — rather than Mark, who holds LPA for property and finances — raises a question about whether the family understood the nature of what was being signed and who should have been the primary signatory. It also illustrates the way responsibilities were distributed between the two attorneys in practice, which is relevant to the selective LPA communication pattern. See Issue 43.

**4. Invoice demanded simultaneously with contract processing**

Lee Forder's email says "the invoice will still need to be paid" in the same breath as confirming receipt of the contract. The DPA had not yet been formally executed — the stated execution date is 19 May 2023, 16 days later. Invoices were being demanded for a contract that had not yet been formally executed, against a care home the resident had already left. See Issues 23 and 25.
