## Significance

**1. DPA being actively progressed in January–February 2023 without MCA compliance**

This chain confirms the Council's finance team was processing the DPA application on 30 January and 3 February 2023. Bridget's incapacity had been formally confirmed since 10 November 2020 (DoLS Standard Authorisation). At no point in this chain — or in any disclosed document — is there any reference to a Best Interests decision, an IMCA referral, or an RPR consultation before the DPA was progressed. The DPA was drafted and executed (19 May 2023) without MCA compliance. See Issue 26.

**2. Lee Forder is a finance officer, not a social worker**

The DPA was being managed entirely by the finance team. The social worker responsible for the care assessment (Jacquie Deacon) was conducting the DPA process in parallel — see the 30 November 2022 email — but the MCA obligations that should have preceded execution of any agreement on behalf of a person lacking capacity are a social care function, not a finance function. There is no evidence the two streams were coordinated or that any social care MCA assessment was conducted.

**3. Property valuation requirements reduced without explanation**

Lee Forder states the Council "would normally require 3 valuations" but will accept 2. This is a reduction of the standard requirement, apparently made by a manager without any recorded reason. The DPA Application Form (signed 16 February 2023) records three property valuations as submitted. The basis on which the Council assessed the property value — and whether it was accurate — is relevant to the lending limit under the DPA and the calculation of charges.

**4. Timing: DPA application active while Bridget is still at Westcliff Lodge**

This exchange is dated 30 January – 3 February 2023. Bridget was still at Westcliff Lodge at this point (she moved to Palmerston House on 11 April 2023). The DPA that was eventually executed on 19 May 2023 named Westcliff Lodge — but the application process was being conducted two to three months before the move, with no mechanism to update the contract when she moved. See Issues 23 and 25.
