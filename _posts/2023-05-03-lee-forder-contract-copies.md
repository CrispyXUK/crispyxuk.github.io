## Significance

**1. Only one of two required contract copies had been returned**

Lee Forder confirms that as of 3 May 2023 only one signed contract had been received. Two copies were required — both signed and witnessed. This means the DPA execution process was incomplete on 3 May 2023. The stated execution date on the DPA is 19 May 2023 — 16 days later. The PDF metadata shows the document was scanned on 17 May 2023. The timeline is therefore: one copy received by approximately 26 April 2023 (per the earlier chain), second copy still outstanding on 3 May 2023, document scanned 17 May 2023, stated execution date 19 May 2023. See Issues 24 and 39.

**2. "Manager has signed them" — Council signature process**

Lee Forder states "once the manager has signed them both one will be returned to you." This confirms the Council's counter-signature was applied after both copies were received — not at the same time as the family signed. The version of the DPA disclosed to Mark after his complaint had a blank Council signature page. The version registered at the Land Registry (CH1) shows the full execution. The process by which the Council's signature was added — after receipt of the family's signed copies — is consistent with the PDF metadata anomaly. See Issue 39.

**3. Mark cannot find the second copy**

Mark's response — "I can't find the other copy, thought it was for record but have a scan of it. Have got it signed up if that's ok?" — suggests he was uncertain about the execution requirements. He was attempting to comply by providing a scanned signed copy rather than the original. This is consistent with the family having been placed under financial and procedural pressure to complete a transaction they did not fully understand, in respect of a care home Bridget had already left. See Issue 23.

**4. This exchange occurred 22 days after Bridget moved to Palmerston House**

At the time of this exchange, Bridget had been at Palmerston House since 11 April 2023 — 22 days. The contract being finalised here named Westcliff Lodge. Neither Lee Forder nor any other Council officer raised any query about the address in the contract despite knowing Bridget had moved. The Council's IPC for Palmerston House had been created on 13 April 2023 — the Council's own records showed the placement change. See Issue 23.
