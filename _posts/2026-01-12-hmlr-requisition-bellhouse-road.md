## Significance

**1. HMLR correspondence — title EX130167**

The Land Registry reference EX130167 corresponds to 84 Bellhouse Road, Leigh-on-Sea — the property against which the Council registered a legal charge (CH1) on 12 June 2023. This correspondence, dated January 2026, confirms ongoing Land Registry activity relating to that title. The nature of the requisition is contained in the attached PDF — a formal requisition, reminder or letter — indicating HMLR had an open matter concerning the property at this point.

**2. Timing — during active complaint handling**

The correspondence arrives on 12 January 2026. The formal complaint was submitted 5 September 2025 and the first response was issued 30 October 2025. The Council's legal charge on the property was in dispute — the DPA was executed naming Westcliff Lodge, not Palmerston House, and the family's position was that all charges accrued at Palmerston lacked contractual authority. HMLR activity at this point is consistent with the Council's charge registration being scrutinised or an associated application being live. See Issues 25 and 39.

**3. The charge was registered against a defective DPA**

The CH1 legal charge registered on 12 June 2023 was based on a DPA that named Westcliff Lodge — a care home Bridget had left 62 days before the charge was registered. No written variation was ever produced to extend the DPA to cover Palmerston House. The charge on 84 Bellhouse Road therefore rests on a DPA with no contractual authority to cover the charges being deferred. The HMLR correspondence is relevant to the question of whether the charge can be maintained, varied or removed in light of the DPA's defects. See Issues 23 and 25.

**4. Address inconsistency relevant to the charge**

The Council's own Individual Placement Contract for Palmerston House names the address as 23-25 Palmerston Road. The CQC registration and the ICB letter give 21 Palmerston Road. These are separately-owned Land Registry titles. The charge is on 84 Bellhouse Road — but the DPA it purports to secure covers a care home whose address is disputed in the Council's own records. See Issue 41.
