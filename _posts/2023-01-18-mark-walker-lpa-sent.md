## Significance

**1. LPA sent to the finance team — not to the social care team**

Mark sent his LPA to Lee Forder, Finance Officer, in January 2023. The LPA was requested in the context of progressing the DPA application. This establishes that the Council's DPA process was being managed entirely by the Finance Team. The MCA obligations that should have preceded execution of any agreement on behalf of a person lacking capacity — Best Interests meeting, IMCA referral, RPR consultation — are social care functions, not finance functions. The Finance Team was processing the DPA without triggering the social care MCA compliance process. See Issue 26.

**2. Scanned copy accepted — certified copy offered but not requested**

Mark offered a certified copy. Lee Forder did not request one. The DPA application form (signed 16 February 2023) records "Enduring or Lasting Power of Attorney" as the basis for Mark's authority. Accepting a scanned uncertified copy for a transaction that would result in a legal charge being placed on a property is inconsistent with the evidential standards that should apply to LPA verification for DPA purposes.

**3. Timing — DPA application in progress**

This email is dated 18 January 2023, one month after the DPA purportedly commenced (19 December 2022) and one month before the DPA application form was signed (16 February 2023). The DPA was already operational before the formal application was completed and before the LPA had been properly verified. The charge commenced deferring amounts from December 2022 — before Mark had even provided his LPA documentation to the team processing the agreement.

**4. "Hi guys" — tone of the exchange**

The casual register of this email, and the Finance Team's handling of the DPA as an administrative process, is consistent with the broader finding that the DPA was treated as a routine financial instrument rather than a transaction requiring MCA compliance and legal formality on behalf of a person who lacked capacity. The DoLS Standard Authorisation confirming Bridget lacked capacity had been in place since November 2020 — over two years before this email.
