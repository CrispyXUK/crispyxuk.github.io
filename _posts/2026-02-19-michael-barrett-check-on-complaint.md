## Significance

**1. The Council coordinated with the Finance Team before issuing new invoices — and checked LGSCO status first**

Michael Barrett's email reveals that the Council's Complaints Team and the Finance Team manager were in communication about whether to issue new invoices, and specifically asked whether Mark had received a reply from the LGSCO before proceeding. This is the Council's complaints officer acting as a conduit between the Finance Team and the complainant to assess the LGSCO position. The appropriate conduct — once a matter has been referred to the LGSCO — is for the Council to engage with the LGSCO process rather than to use the complainant as an intelligence source. See Issue 29 and compare the Pearl Adams email of 26 May 2026 (same pattern).

**2. "We have not received any contact from them"**

Barrett states the Council has not received any contact from the LGSCO at the point of this email (17 February 2026). This is consistent with the LGSCO timeline — the case was not formally accepted until 18 May 2026. The Council was checking whether LGSCO involvement had formalised before deciding whether to issue new invoices. The implication is that the Council would have proceeded differently if the LGSCO had already been formally engaged.

**3. The complaint had not been properly resolved — and the Council knew it**

Barrett acknowledges Mark had contacted the Council "to say that you felt there were some aspects of the finance issues that had not been addressed." The first complaint response (30 October 2025) had been issued but was incomplete. Barrett's email confirms the Council was aware, as of February 2026, that the financial issues remained unresolved — yet the second complaint response (11 March 2026) also failed to address them substantively. See Issue 42.

**4. Mark's response — 17 February 2026 Care Act email**

Mark's substantive reply to Barrett's enquiry (sent 17 February 2026 at 23:15) set out the central Care Act argument in detail — the absence of s.9, s.13 and s.25 compliance, the safeguarding history, the NHS/EPUT records gap, and the DoLS. That email, sent the same evening Barrett wrote, was the basis on which the second disclosure bundle was assembled and issued on 11 March 2026.
