## Significance

**1. DPA contract not yet drafted as of 30 January 2023 — but charges were already running**

Lee Forder states on 30 January 2023: "Once we receive the building insurance documents, we can send off to have the contract drafted." This confirms the DPA contract had not yet been drafted on 30 January 2023. Yet the DPA purportedly commenced on 19 December 2022 — 42 days before this email. Charges were being deferred and accumulated against a contract that had not yet been drafted at the point of the deferred amounts accruing. The DPA was operational in practice before it existed as a document. See Issue 24.

**2. The DPA application form was not signed until 16 February 2023**

The DPA Application Form carries Mark's signature dated 16 February 2023 — 17 days after this exchange. The sequence is: DPA commences 19 December 2022 → contract still not drafted 30 January 2023 → application form signed 16 February 2023 → contract eventually executed with a stated date of 19 May 2023 (PDF scanned 17 May 2023). The DPA was operational for five months before it was formally executed. See Issues 24 and 25.

**3. Lee Forder is handling DPA compliance requirements — not social care**

The buildings insurance requirement is a standard DPA condition under Care and Support Statutory Guidance. Lee Forder is checking DPA administrative compliance (insurance, valuations) with no reference to MCA compliance requirements. The DPA was being progressed as a financial administrative process. No social care officer was coordinating the required MCA Best Interests decision, IMCA referral or RPR consultation. See Issue 26.

**4. "I can forward the estimate for the house from Approach Estates"**

Mark's casual reference to forwarding an estate agent's estimate confirms the property valuation process was being handled informally by email rather than through a formal valuation process. The DPA Application Form records three property valuations as having been provided, but the process of obtaining them was coordinated between Mark and the Finance Team without any formal independent oversight.
