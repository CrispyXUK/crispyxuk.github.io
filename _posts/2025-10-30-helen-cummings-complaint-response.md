## Significance

**1. The complaint was referred to a social care team manager by the finance team**

Helen Cummings states she is responding "on behalf of the Finance Team, who have asked me to respond due to the social care reference included in your concerns." This is the first indication that the complaint had been internally redirected. Mark's complaint was submitted to Social Care Income (Finance), but the formal response was issued by the Ageing Well West Team Manager — the same team that conducted the CHC checklists and ASC assessments. The Finance Team did not respond to the financial and contractual issues raised.

**2. This response fails to address the core complaint**

The attached letter (issued the same day) addressed only the CHC history and directed Mark to the LGSCO if dissatisfied. It made no reference to:
- The DPA being executed naming the wrong care home
- The absence of MCA compliance in the DPA
- The financial overcharging (£127.59/week at Palmerston; £61.54/week at Westcliff Lodge)
- The failure to apply the mandatory property disregard
- The absence of a needs assessment, care plan or review at the Palmerston transfer
- The double billing and £9,496.79 refund
- Any DoLS or MCA failures

Mark's formal letter of 22 November 2025 set out all of these failures in detail. The Council then issued a second response on 11 March 2026 — also failing to address most of them adequately — before directing him to the LGSCO. See Issue 42.

**3. Helen Cummings works Tuesday to Friday**

A minor but notable detail: Helen Cummings's signature confirms she works Tuesday to Friday. The complaint response was issued on Thursday 30 October 2025. Her working pattern is relevant to the question of responsiveness and availability during the complaint handling period.

**4. The response came from the same team responsible for the CHC assessments**

Helen Cummings is Team Manager of Ageing Well West — the same team as Lynne Gardiner (who completed CHC Checklists #5 and #6 and the July 2025 Care and Support Plan Review). The complaint about the CHC checklists was therefore investigated and responded to by the team that produced them. This is an inherent conflict of interest in the complaint handling process. See Issue 43.
