## Significance

**1. Council confirms Palmerston move date as 11 April 2023 — in writing**

Lynn Hyam's email of 28 August 2025 states explicitly: "Following her move to Palmerston Court on 11th April 2023, the weekly rate was £750.00." This is the Council's own finance team confirming in writing the date Bridget moved to Palmerston House. The DPA signed 19 May 2023 — 38 days later — names Westcliff Lodge as the care home. The Council cannot now dispute the move date. See Issue 23.

**2. Council confirms contractual rate with Palmerston was £750/week**

Lynn Hyam confirms the rate at Palmerston was £750/week from 11 April 2023. The financial assessment letter dated 27 March 2023 states a total of £877.59/week. The difference — £127.59/week — is the overcharge. This email is the Council's own written confirmation of the two figures. The overcharge is now proven from the Council's own correspondence without reference to any external document. See Issue 19.

**3. Property disregard refusal states wrong legal basis**

Lynn Hyam writes: "The property was not subject to mandatory disregard in the financial assessment, as you are not over 60 years of age or a dependent child under 18." This conflates two separate provisions of Schedule 2 to the Care and Support (Charging and Assessment of Resources) Regulations 2014. The disregard Mark relied upon was for a dependent child in occupation — not the over-60 provision. The Council refused the disregard on the wrong legal basis. See Issues 21 and 22.

**4. Council refuses to provide care home contracts — but relies on them for the charge**

Lynn Hyam states the Council cannot provide copies of care home contracts "as these documents contain information relating to all residents." Yet the entire basis for the DPA and the weekly charge is the care home contract. The Council is simultaneously refusing to disclose the contract while relying on it to justify the charges it is demanding. The Council's own Individual Placement Contract (created 13 April 2023) subsequently obtained confirms the £750/week contractual rate, directly contradicting the £877.59 being invoiced. See Issue 19.

**5. Capital below £14,250 confirmed**

Lynn Hyam confirms: "The Financial Assessment Team has not applied a tariff income as Mrs Walker's capital is below £14,250." This confirms that at the time of Council involvement, Bridget's capital had already fallen below the lower threshold — meaning she should not have been treated as a self-funder requiring a DPA. See Issue 20.
