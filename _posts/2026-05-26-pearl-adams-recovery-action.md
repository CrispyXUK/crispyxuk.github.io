## Significance

**1. Recovery action continued after LGSCO referral**

Mark submitted the formal complaint on 5 September 2025. The Council issued its second final response on 11 March 2026. Pearl Adams's chasing email is dated 24 March 2026 — 13 days after the second response, at a point when the matter was clearly headed to the Ombudsman. The Council continued to pursue payment of a disputed and unreconciled debt during the complaint resolution process and immediately after it was exhausted. See Issue 29.

**2. Council required LGSCO reference before suspending recovery**

The Council's position — that it would only suspend recovery action on receipt of the LGSCO reference number — is unusual. Once a complaint has been referred to the LGSCO, the standard expectation under the Local Government Ombudsman framework is that recovery action on disputed amounts is suspended. The Council treated the LGSCO referral as insufficient and required verification. Mark provided the reference (25019572) on 10 April 2026.

**3. Council sought intelligence on the active investigation — 26 May 2026**

Pearl Adams's email of 26 May 2026 — sent eight days after the LGSCO formally accepted the case on 18 May 2026 — asks Mark to provide "an update from the ombudsman in regards to your complaint." The LGSCO investigation was active and the Council was a respondent. The Council's finance team was seeking information about the status of an investigation in which the Council itself was under scrutiny. This is at minimum an inappropriate contact; at worst it represents an attempt to obtain information about the investigation through the complainant rather than through the formal LGSCO process.

**4. "Palmerstone" — consistent spelling error**

Pearl Adams refers to "Palmerstone Care Home" throughout this chain. The correct name is Palmerston House. This is the same error that appears in other Council financial correspondence. It is a minor point but consistent with the pattern of data inaccuracy across Council documents in this case.

**5. No reconciliation ever provided**

Mark's letter of 27 March 2026 explicitly requested "a proper itemised reconciliation of the account, including all invoices, credit notes, payments received, dates, and the legal and financial basis upon which the balance claimed has been calculated." No such reconciliation was provided in response to this chain. The Council suspended recovery action but did not address the substantive dispute about the figures.
