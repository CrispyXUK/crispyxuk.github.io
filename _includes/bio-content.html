A biography of Bridget Penelope Walker, written by her daughter Donna Evans. Included to give the LGSCO investigator a picture of who Bridget is as a person — her history, personality, and the life she lived before her dementia diagnosis.

Early life

Bridget was born on 8 October 1950 to Patricia (Pat) and Keith Warren. As a child she lived in Jamaica with her family, where her father Keith worked for Cable and Wireless. After returning to England the family settled at Great Wheatley Road, Rayleigh. Bridget's sister Jayne Moakes still lives in Rayleigh — and the family's connection to that area is one reason Bridget had a habit of walking from Eastwood toward Rayleigh: she knew that road.

Marriage and family

Bridget married James Walker in January 1980. Their wedding reception was held at her parents' house in Great Wheatley Road. Jim and Bridget enjoyed many holidays abroad and, in later life when the children had grown up, went on a number of cruises together.

James Walker passed away in November 2011. Around two to three years after his death, Bridget received her early-onset Alzheimer's diagnosis.

Bridget has three biological children:

  • Mark Walker — born 1980
  • Donna Evans and Kira Walker — twins, born at home in Eastwood in 1982

She also has two stepchildren from Jim's first marriage to Pauline: Michael Walker and Debbie Razza.

Papua New Guinea and the pantomime

In 1987 the family relocated to Papua New Guinea for eighteen months, following Jim's work with Cleanaway. During that time Bridget performed in the local pantomime — singing and dancing. She loved the stage.

Career and interests

Bridget was a dancer in her younger years and spent time living in Barcelona where she worked as a professional dancer. Back in England she worked as a splash dance instructor and swimming instructor, a florist, and a skilled dressmaker — she made bridesmaid dresses for friends and family.

Grandchildren

Bridget has seven grandchildren:

  • Emily (25), Bekah (22), and Alice — daughters of her stepdaughter. Alice passed away in 2017 at the age of fifteen.
  • Harley (11) and Aiden (6) — sons of Donna Evans, living in Australia.
  • Alex (3) — son of Mark and Vikki Walker, living in Eastwood.
  • Enis (1) — child of Kira Walker, living nearby in Eastwood.

Health background

Provided for clinical and assessment context.

Current conditions at time of bio: Bridget was under the care of Southend Hospital for a full uterine prolapse, managed with a Galhorne pessary, with a review appointment pending.

Surgical history: 2003 knee reconstruction; repair of a double hernia.

Family medical history: Her father had dementia and heart disease — quadruple heart bypass and mini-stroke — and passed away at age 93. Her mother passed approximately twenty years ago following a massive stroke in her early seventies. Whether Bridget has been monitored for cardiovascular or cholesterol issues is not known to the family.

At Sweyne Court: Donna notes that Sweyne Court were treating Bridget for a foot infection at the time of writing, though the family had not been told the diagnosis.

Preferences

  • Food: Not a fan of beef. Prefers chicken, pork, and prawns. Sweet tooth.
  • No known allergies at time of writing.
