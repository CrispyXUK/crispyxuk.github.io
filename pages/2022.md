<p class="lead">Analysis of Bridget's GP and NHS records for 2022. Covers the final months of self-funded placement at Admiral Court (Jan–Sept), transfer to Westcliff Lodge (late Sept 2022), and the first CHC checklist (26 Oct 2022). The 31 August 2022 EPUT annual dementia review — eight weeks before Checklist 1 — is the most evidentially significant record of the year: it documents advanced cognitive impairment, prolapsed bowel pain, sores on feet from continuous walking, and moderate frailty. Checklist 1 found threshold not met.</p>
