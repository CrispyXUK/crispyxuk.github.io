<h3>April 2023 — Transfer to Palmerston House</h3>
<ul>
  <li><strong>07 Apr:</strong> Telephone referral to EPUT SEE Dementia Intense Support (DIST); notes Alzheimer's Dementia diagnosed 2014, previously on Donepezil 10mg and Promethazine. <strong>No physical health check since 2019</strong> — a 4-year gap documented on admission <a class="issue-badge" href="{{ '/issues/' | relative_url }}#issue-8">#8</a></li>
  <li><strong>11 Apr:</strong> <strong>Bridget admitted to Palmerston House</strong> from Westcliff Lodge. Rapidly presenting with challenging behaviours: refusing personal care, aggression toward residents and staff, inappropriate touching. GP ordered FBC and MSU to rule out UTI. <strong>No Council needs assessment or care plan produced at point of transfer.</strong> <a class="issue-badge issue-badge-high" href="{{ '/issues/' | relative_url }}#issue-30">#30</a></li>
  <li><strong>17 Apr:</strong> DIST Care Home Team involved; referral from care home requesting support — patient "quite a handful", causing disruption. Behaviours: unaware of surroundings, resistant to personal care, attempting escape, trashing items, struck a resident. DIST to assess.</li>
  <li><strong>18 Apr:</strong> Southend Medical Centre home visit (GP). Patient seen eating lunch, "new to home very disruptive to other residents." Full bloods and MSU taken with consent. Large protruding uterovaginal prolapse noted (diagnosed 2003). Comprehensive blood work: Hb 123, CRP 1.0, glucose 5.0, LFTs normal, thyroid normal, bone profile normal.</li>
  <li><strong>20 Apr:</strong> GP comments: "pessary no longer an option due to dementia." Discusses potential surgical management given patient "extremely unsettled". References June 2022 gynaecology letter.</li>
  <li><strong>21 Apr:</strong> <strong>Consent/confidentiality breach:</strong> Large prolapse photographed and intimate image sent without consent. GP issued urgent instruction to remove from all records immediately. Urgent surgical referral sent. <a class="issue-badge issue-badge-high" href="{{ '/issues/' | relative_url }}#issue-26">#26</a></li>
  <li><strong>25 Apr:</strong> Ward round — resident reported well</li>
</ul>

<h3>May 2023 — Hip Fracture</h3>
<ul>
  <li><strong>03 May:</strong> DIST Annual Dementia Review: patient can talk but cannot make meaningful conversation; fully mobile; staff concerned with physically aggressive behaviour toward staff and residents; sleeps well; advanced dementia; no memory recall. <strong>Plan: Start Memantine 5mg titrated over 4 weeks (5mg → 20mg at night). Start Promethazine 25mg TDS for behaviour management.</strong> Care home to complete behaviour chart.</li>
  <li><strong>11 May:</strong> <strong>FALL INCIDENT</strong> — patient fell from chair while mobilising; unwitnessed but captured on camera. Right hip/knee bruising, swelling, vomited twice. PCN assessment: no LOC, no head injury, pain on movement, managed to weight-bear with pain. <strong>DNACPR form completed (Dr Valliattu) on same day.</strong></li>
  <li><strong>11 May:</strong> EPUT Urgent Community Response called; patient vomited ×2; advised monitoring, paracetamol, liaison with GP if continued vomiting.</li>
  <li><strong>15 May:</strong> <strong>HOSPITAL ADMISSION — Southend University Hospital (Shopland Ward)</strong>. X-rays: <strong>displaced subcapital fracture right neck of femur (NOF)</strong>; right knee: degenerative changes, no fracture. Emergency <strong>right hip cemented Zimmer/Mueller hemiarthroplasty</strong> performed under Mr Packer. Discharged with Memantine titration pack.</li>
  <li><strong>25–26 May:</strong> Discharge from hospital. <strong>Hospital lost Memantine and Promethazine medications and essential paperwork during admission.</strong> Care home requested further supplies; confusion about whether to restart Memantine from 5mg or continue from 10mg already titrated. <strong>Critical gap: approximately 10 days without Memantine during hospitalisation.</strong> <strong>No CHC assessment at discharge despite hospitalisation, NOF fracture, and advanced dementia — required under National Framework paras 63–64.</strong> <a class="issue-badge issue-badge-high" href="{{ '/issues/' | relative_url }}#issue-9">#9</a></li>
  <li><strong>19 May:</strong> <strong>DPA signed at Westcliff Lodge</strong> — Bridget had moved to Palmerston House 38 days earlier on 11 April 2023. <a class="issue-badge issue-badge-high" href="{{ '/issues/' | relative_url }}#issue-23">#23</a></li>
  <li><strong>29 May:</strong> DIST discharged from care; discharge plan added to care home hospital discharges list.</li>
  <li><strong>30 May:</strong> Care home requests crushed/liquid medication; patient spitting out tablets.</li>
</ul>
