<h3>February 2021</h3>
<ul>
  <li><strong>02 Feb:</strong> GP home visit at Admiral Court; Promethazine PRN discontinued (not administered; patient not agitated; mobile, eating/drinking well)</li>
  <li><strong>04 Feb:</strong> COVID-19 vaccine — first dose (AstraZeneca Vaxzevria)</li>

</ul>

<h2 id="evidential">Evidential Value — Summary</h2>

<p>2021 represents a critical transition year. <strong>Jan–Apr:</strong> Care at Admiral Court; Promethazine discontinued; advance care planning completed; family engaged and consenting. <strong>May–Jul:</strong> Uterine prolapse requiring active gynae management; continence escalation; wound care episode; Waterlow scores rising. <strong>Aug–Dec:</strong> Routine vaccinations; COVID-19 screening; no major escalations documented.</p>
