<h2 id="self-funded">Self-Funded Placement: The Council's Absence</h2>

<div style="background:#FFF2CC;border-left:4px solid #C55A11;padding:10px 14px;border-radius:0 6px 6px 0;margin:1em 0;font-size:.9rem">
  <strong>Both placements were entirely self-funded — the Council did not engage.</strong> Neither Sweyne Court (respite) nor Admiral Court (permanent) involved any Council needs assessment, financial assessment, or care planning. The family arranged and funded both placements privately because the Council did not respond to them during this critical period. 
  
  <strong>There is no Council-produced care plan, eligibility determination, or contribution assessment for either placement. The absence of these documents is itself evidential — it is not that they were lost; they were never produced.</strong> This self-funded arrangement is the direct consequence of the Council's unlawful refusal to assess. <a class="issue-badge issue-badge-high" href="{{ '/issues/' | relative_url }}#issue-20">#20</a> <a class="issue-badge" href="{{ '/issues/' | relative_url }}#issue-31">#31</a>

<h2 id="unlawful">Unlawful Refusal to Assess: The Core Complaint</h2>

<div style="background:#FCE4D6;border-left:4px solid #C00000;padding:10px 14px;border-radius:0 6px 6px 0;margin:1em 0;font-size:.9rem">
  <strong>The Council's complete absence from the 2020 care pathway represents unlawful failure to assess under Care Act 2014 s.13.</strong>
  
  <ul style="margin:.4em 0 0">
    <li><strong>Triggering event:</strong> Bridget admitted to permanent care home (15 Sept) — an objective triggering event for statutory needs assessment</li>
    <li><strong>Clinical complexity:</strong> Cellulitis, behavioral crisis, continence issues, cognitive decline, nursing home placement, Waterlow escalation</li>
    <li><strong>Family status:</strong> Daughters hold Lasting Power of Attorney (LPA); actively seeking Council support</li>
    <li><strong>Council response:</strong> <strong>Silence</strong> — no assessment, no communication, no financial offer throughout 2020</li>
    <li><strong>Consequence:</strong> Family forced to self-fund Admiral Court fees at <strong>private nursing home rates</strong> — substantially higher than Council-funded care would cost</li>
    <li><strong>Statutory duty breach:</strong> Care Act 2014 s.13–14 (duty to assess when person appears to have care needs) was not triggered</li>
    <li><strong>Belated response:</strong> DPA signed 19 May 2023 — Council's <strong>first documented acknowledgment of financial responsibility (45 months late)</strong></li>
  </ul>
</div>
