## Ethnicity Misclassification {#ethnicity}

The GP record contains a recurring erroneous flag on blood test results across three clinical episodes:

| Date | Test | Flag text |
|---|---|---|
| 18 Apr 2023 | Full blood panel (dementia review) | "EGFR ETHNICITY CORRECTION FACT: NICE no longer recommends adjusting eGFR for African-Caribbeans (NG203, updated August 2021)." |
| 6 Feb 2024 | eGFR (unwell episode) | Same flag — classified as African-Caribbean |
| 8 Aug 2025 | eGFR (medication monitoring) | Same flag — classified as African-Caribbean |

Bridget is **White British**. This is confirmed by the DIST dementia review record of 30 Apr 2024 which records "White British — ethnic category 2001 census". The EGFR African-Caribbean classification has no clinical basis and has persisted across three separate laboratories over two years, indicating the misclassification is embedded in the patient's demographic record and propagated to clinical systems.

The Council's 11 Mar 2026 complaint response letter was the first occasion on which this was raised with the Council. No confirmation of correction has been received. The questions raised in the 17 Feb 2026 letter to the Council remain unanswered:
- Where the incorrect classification originated
- Whether it appears within Council assessment records
- Whether it has been shared across NHS and social care systems
- What steps have been taken to rectify the record

## Covert Medication — 13-Month Gap Without MCA {#covert}

The SAR documents a critical sequence establishing that covert medication administration was occurring at Palmerston House from at least May 2023 without a completed Mental Capacity Act assessment:

| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| 30 May 2023 | Care home manager emails GP surgery requesting permission to crush Bridget's medication covertly. Reason: "she spits them out." |
| 25 Aug 2023 | Pharmacist medication review notes: "Drug compliance poor — often spits out, covert policy in place." Records no signed MCA1 form. |
| 16 Jul 2024 | PCN ward round documents: "Care home manager mentions having no copy of this [MCA] in patient's notes which caused a deduction from previous CQC inspection." |
| **24 Jul 2024** | **First formal MCA assessment completed (Dr Mrityunjay Khopkar).** Authorisation to crush/open medication form signed. |
| 14 Aug 2024 | Care home manager signs authorisation form. |
| Aug 2024 | Surgery contacts care home to collect unsigned covert medication paperwork. |
| Jul 2025 | Second MCA required (Dr Win Aung, 29 Jul 2025) because the completing GP left the practice without ensuring the care home received a copy — a CQC inspection finding. |

**The gap between the start of covert administration (May 2023) and the first completed MCA (July 2024) is over 13 months.** The GP record confirms covert administration was ongoing during this entire period without a completed MCA process — a fundamental breach of the Mental Capacity Act 2005.

The Deferred Payment Agreement was signed 19 May 2023 — during this period of unlawful covert administration. The DPA requires that the supported person has capacity or that a proper best interests process has been followed. The MCA1 was not completed until over a year later.

## Hip Fracture — 15 May 2023 {#fracture}

The SAR confirms the following sequence:

- **11 May 2023**: PCN ward round records unwitnessed fall. Bruising right hip and knee; Bridget vomited twice. X-ray form issued. DNAR signed same day.
- **11 May 2023**: EPUT Urgent Community Response attends. Care home reports patient fell, was able to mobilise but in pain. Ambulance not called initially.
- **15 May 2023 16:27**: Admitted to Shopland Ward (7th floor, Tower Block) under Mr Packer. X-ray: "displaced subcapital fracture of the right neck of femur."
- **15 May 2023**: Emergency right hip cemented Zimmer/Mueller hemiarthroplasty.
- **25 May 2023**: Discharge summary issued.
- **26 May 2023**: GP notes that Bridget spent 10 days in hospital without Memantine (hospital did not administer titration pack brought in by home; pack lost). Promethazine also lost in hospital.
- **27 May 2023**: East of England Ambulance falls notification — records patient had "recent NOF surgery" at time of a further fall on 27 May 2023.

No CHC checklist was completed at hospital discharge. No post-fracture Care Act review under s.27 has been disclosed.

## EPUT NHS Records — Discrepancy {#eput}

The Council's complaint response and the Mark Walker email of 17 Feb 2026 both note that **NHS and EPUT were contacted and confirmed they hold no records relating to Bridget prior to 2025.**

The SAR directly contradicts this:

- **Sep 2020**: EPUT DIST (Dementia Intensive Support Team) extensively involved — multiple calls, CMHT crisis briefing, placement at Admiral Court monitored.
- **Nov 2020**: EPUT Integrated Adult Services — Waterlow assessment.
- **Nov–Dec 2020**: EPUT Adult Bladder & Bowel — continence assessment.
- **Feb–Mar 2021**: EPUT Integrated Adult Services — wound care, skin tear left forearm.
- **Aug 2022**: EPUT DIST — annual dementia review at Admiral Court.
- **Oct 2022 – ongoing**: EPUT Adult Bladder & Bowel — continence at Westcliff Lodge then Palmerston.
- **Apr 2023**: EPUT DIST — involvement at Palmerston from day of transfer.
- **Jan 2024**: EPUT Integrated Adult Services and EPUT Tissue Viability — DTI management.
- **Feb–onwards 2024**: EPUT Palliative Care — ongoing involvement.

Either the claim that no records are held pre-2025 is incorrect, or there has been a catastrophic failure of record retention and inter-agency information sharing. In either case, the EPUT records referenced in the SAR should be obtainable.
