<p class="lead">Analysis of Bridget's GP and NHS records for 2024. The year opens with a severe constipation crisis requiring three types of enema and a deep tissue injury (DTI) to the left buttock (January). In February, rapid clinical deterioration prompts emergency palliative care referral — Bridget transitions from chairbound to fully bedbound within weeks. Waterlow scores escalate from 17 to 24 across the year. A formal MCA assessment for covert medication is belatedly completed in July 2024, 14 months after covert administration was already documented.</p>
