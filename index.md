<h2>Background</h2>
<p>Mrs Bridget Penelope Walker (born 08 October 1950) resides at Palmerston House, Palmerston Road, Westcliff-on-Sea. She has advanced dementia, lacks capacity, and has been subject to DoLS authorisation since November 2020. She is bedbound, non-verbal, doubly incontinent, requires 2-staff hoist transfers, has a Waterlow score of 27, covert medication, and a DNACPR.</p>
<p>Mark Walker and Kira Walker hold joint Lasting Power of Attorney across both domains. A third sibling, Donna Walker, is in Australia.</p>
<p>Bridget entered Admiral Court (a registered nursing home) in September 2020, initially as respite, becoming permanent due to Covid-19. The family self-funded £1,450/week for two years with no FNC and no CHC referral, depleting capital from above £23,250 to below £14,250. She moved to Westcliff Lodge (residential) in September 2022 and to Palmerston House (residential, CQC-prohibited from providing nursing care) in April 2023.</p>
<p>A formal complaint was submitted on 5 September 2025. Two Council final responses followed (Oct 2025, Mar 2026). The LGSCO accepted the case on 18 May 2026 (Ref {{ site.case_ref_lgsco }}, Investigator {{ site.investigator }}).</p>

<h2>Strongest evidential strands</h2>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Document integrity</strong> — CHC checklists and assessments record a future care home address as current; logically impossible unless created later and backdated. <a href="{{ '/document-integrity/' | relative_url }}">See analysis →</a></li>
  <li><strong>Proven overcharge</strong> — the Council's own placement contract shows it paid £750/week while invoicing the family £877.59/week.</li>
  <li><strong>Property disregard</strong> — the DPA application form proves dependent occupants were declared in February 2023, rebutting the Council's refusal.</li>
  <li><strong>FNC failure</strong> — no NHS-Funded Nursing Care assessment during the Admiral Court nursing home placement.</li>
  <li><strong>Placement appropriateness</strong> — nursing-level needs at a residential home prohibited from providing nursing care.</li>
</ul>
